The law in Ashburn Creek is a living, breathing part of the community — not a background system. Lawmen walk the same muddy streets as outlaws, lawyers argue cases in front of whoever will listen, and justice is often decided by the strength of your evidence as much as the barrel of a gun. These roles sit at the heart of the server's story, which is why they are whitelisted and require staff approval before you step into them.

## Whitelisted Status & Queue Priority

Law enforcement and legal roles are **whitelisted positions**. You must receive staff approval through a support ticket before you begin playing in an official law or legal capacity. Once approved, your account receives **queue priority**, meaning you move to the front of the line when the server is full. Visit the [Queue Priority](/getting-started/queue-priority) page to understand how that system works.

## Lawman / Sheriff's Deputy

As a lawman or sheriff's deputy, you are responsible for maintaining order in and around Ashburn Creek. That means responding to crimes in progress, conducting arrests, gathering evidence, and upholding the rule of law — even when the rule of law is inconvenient for you personally.

Core responsibilities as a lawman:

- Respond to reported crimes and disturbances in a timely, in-character manner
- Conduct arrests with proper roleplay — announce your authority, give suspects a chance to comply, and use force only when necessary
- Escort prisoners, process them, and coordinate with lawyers where applicable
- Document notable incidents so that ongoing story threads remain consistent
- Prioritise roleplay over gunplay at all times — create interaction before conflict, and never draw iron when words would serve better

## Lawyer

Frontier justice needs advocates. As a lawyer, you represent clients in disputes, argue cases before available authority figures, and help navigate a legal system that is still finding its footing on the edge of civilisation.

Core responsibilities as a lawyer:

- Accept clients and represent their interests in legal proceedings
- Gather testimony, review evidence, and construct arguments appropriate to the era
- Work alongside — and sometimes against — lawmen to reach fair outcomes
- Create legal storylines that involve multiple players and generate lasting consequences

## Corruption

Frontier lawmen and lawyers were not always clean. Corruption is a legitimate and dramatic story thread in Ashburn Creek — but it must be handled carefully to protect the experience of everyone involved.

<Warning>
  **Playing a corrupt lawman or lawyer requires prior staff approval via a support ticket.** Do not begin corruption storylines, accept bribes, or act against your sworn duties without receiving explicit approval first. Unapproved corruption will be treated as a rule violation. Submit a ticket, outline your story idea, and wait for staff confirmation before proceeding.
</Warning>

When corruption is approved, play it with subtlety and purpose. The best corruption arcs create tension and story for the whole community — not just an excuse to abuse your authority.

## Vigilantism

If you are not playing a whitelisted law role, stay out of active enforcement scenes.

The server rules are clear: **do not interfere with active law enforcement scenes or criminal conflicts you are not part of.** This applies to well-meaning bystanders as much as it does to outlaws. If you witness an arrest in progress, let it play out. If you believe the law acted wrongly, pursue that grievance through legitimate in-character channels — or apply for a law role yourself.

## How to Apply

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the Rules">
    Before applying, read the [Roleplay Rules](/rules/roleplay-rules) and [Community Standards](/rules/community-standards) in full. Law roles are held to a high standard, and staff will expect you to know the rules before you wear a badge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Develop Your Character">
    Have a clear sense of who your lawman or lawyer is before you apply. A character with a backstory, a motivation, and a voice makes for stronger applications and better roleplay. Visit [Creating Your Character](/getting-started/creating-your-character) if you need a starting point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit a Support Ticket">
    Open a support ticket in the Discord server. In your ticket, introduce your character, describe the role you want to fill (deputy, sheriff, lawyer), and briefly explain the kind of stories you want to create. Staff will review your application and follow up with any questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await Staff Approval">
    Staff review applications on a case-by-case basis. Do not begin playing in an official law or legal capacity until you receive explicit written approval. Once approved, you will receive queue priority and any relevant in-game access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ride Out">
    Pin on your badge or hang out your shingle. Introduce yourself to the community through roleplay, and start building the stories that make Ashburn Creek worth protecting.
  </Step>
</Steps>
