Ashburn Creek is built around character-driven work and the stories that grow from it. Every rider who arrives in town brings a past, a set of skills, and — eventually — a place in the community. Your livelihood is not just a job; it is the engine of your character's story, the reason you interact with strangers, and the thread that ties you to the world around you. Whether you pin on a badge, hang out a doctor's shingle, or simply drift through town trading pelts and tall tales, what you do shapes who you become.

## How Roles Work

Roles in Ashburn Creek emerge through roleplay rather than menus or instant unlocks. You do not claim a livelihood — you build it. Talk to people in town, establish your skills through scenes, and let your reputation develop naturally. Some roles, however, carry elevated responsibilities and therefore require a formal step before you can fill them.

<Note>
  **Whitelisted roles — including law enforcement, lawyer, and doctor — require staff approval before you begin playing in that capacity.** Submit a ticket through the Discord support system to express your interest. Approved whitelisted roles also grant you **queue priority**, helping you get into the server faster during busy periods. See the [Queue Priority](/getting-started/queue-priority) page for details.
</Note>

## Role Categories

Ashburn Creek's livelihoods fall into four broad families. Each offers a different way to engage with the world and with other players.

**Law & Order** — Sheriffs, deputies, and frontier lawyers keep the peace and navigate a justice system that is often as rough as the land itself. These roles are whitelisted and carry firm expectations around conduct and corruption.

**Medicine** — Doctors and field medics treat wounds, manage medical scenes, and serve as an essential lifeline during conflicts and accidents. This role is whitelisted and demands honest, era-appropriate roleplay.

**Commerce & Trade** — Merchants, bartenders, general store owners, and traveling traders form the economic backbone of the community. These roles are open to all players and reward creativity and consistency.

**Independent & Frontier Work** — Ranchers, hunters, trappers, gamblers, drifters, and craftspeople carve out a living on the edge of civilisation. These roles need no approval and are often the best place to start your story.

## Find Your Place

You do not need to arrive in Ashburn Creek with a plan. Many players begin as drifters or independent workers and discover their niche through the people they meet and the situations they stumble into. Start simple, create scenes, and let your character's story point the way.
