Your inventory holds everything your character carries — food, ammunition, crafting materials, documents, and more. Keeping it organized means you spend less time fumbling through menus and more time living your frontier story. This page covers how to open your inventory, split item stacks, pick up items from the world, and what to do when something is not working the way you expect.

## Opening Your Inventory

Press `I` at any time to open your inventory. Use the on-screen navigation to browse your items, equip gear, consume provisions, or inspect what you are carrying. Press `I` again or tap `Escape` to close it.

<Tip>
  Need to pick up an item you see in the world? Open the **Interaction Menu** with `Caps Lock` (or `Middle Mouse Wheel`) while standing near the item and select the pick-up option from the radial menu.
</Tip>

## Splitting Item Stacks

When you have more than one of the same stackable item — bullets, herbs, coin pouches — you can divide the stack to give a portion to another player or stow items separately. The split option only appears on eligible stacked items that contain more than one unit.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your inventory">
    Press `I` to open your inventory screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the stack you want to divide">
    Navigate to the item stack. It must contain at least two of the same item for the split option to appear. Single items cannot be divided.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the split action">
    With the stack selected, look for the split action in the item's context menu. Select it to open the split interface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your amount">
    Enter or drag to the quantity you want to split off. Confirm the action to divide the stack into two separate stacks in your inventory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### If the Split Option Is Missing

Not every item in Ashburn Creek can be split — some are intentionally locked as single units by design. Before assuming there is a bug, try these checks:

- **Confirm the stack has more than one item.** A stack of exactly one shows no split option by design.
- **Some items cannot be split at all.** Unique items, quest items, and certain equipment are non-divisible regardless of quantity.
- **Close and reopen your inventory.** If the interface loaded in an unexpected state, a fresh open often refreshes the available actions.

If you have confirmed the stack is greater than one, the item type is normally splittable, and the option still does not appear after reopening your inventory — take a screenshot and open a support ticket in the Ashburn Creek Discord so staff can investigate.

<Note>
  Items and money **cannot be transferred between your own characters**. Each character you create on Ashburn Creek maintains a fully separate inventory and wallet. This is a server rules requirement, not a technical limitation — attempting to use a third party to move items between your own characters is a violation of the rules.
</Note>

## General Inventory Tips

Staying organized on the frontier takes a little habit-building. Keep these tips in mind as you play:

- **Check weight regularly.** Your character can only carry so much before movement and stamina are affected. Drop or store non-essential items at your camp or in a rented room when you are heading into demanding territory.
- **Use the Hotbar for quick access.** Press `U` to open your Hotbar and assign frequently used items — medical supplies, lanterns, food — so you can reach them without opening the full inventory mid-scene.
- **Loot deliberately.** Not everything worth picking up is obvious. Use the Interaction Menu (`Caps Lock`) near bodies, containers, and world objects to see what is available before committing to a pick-up.
- **Store valuables before risky situations.** If you are heading into a situation where your character might be downed or robbed as part of a scene, consider what your character would realistically carry versus leave behind.
- **Provisions matter.** Keep a modest supply of food and water on hand. Your character's health and stamina cores drain over time and during physical activity — restoring them in the field is far easier when you are already prepared.

## Inventory Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| Inventory opens but appears empty | Wait a moment — the server may still be loading your character data. Close and reopen with `I`. |
| Item I picked up is not showing | Check the correct inventory tab or category filter. Some items sort into specific sections. |
| Cannot interact with inventory at all | Confirm your `I` keybind in RedM settings. See [Keybinds](/gameplay/keybinds) if it has been remapped. |
| Eligible stack will not split after all checks | Screenshot the stack details and open a support ticket. |

For connection and loading issues that might affect your inventory loading correctly, see the [Troubleshooting Common Issues](/support/troubleshooting) guide.
