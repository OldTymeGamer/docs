If you're new to Ashburn Creek or just getting started with RedM, you'll find answers to the most common questions below. Read through these before reaching out to staff — chances are your question is already covered here.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="What platform is Ashburn Creek on?">
    Ashburn Creek runs on **RedM**, the multiplayer modification framework for Red Dead Redemption 2. You need a legitimate copy of Red Dead Redemption 2 installed through Rockstar Games Launcher, along with the RedM client, before you can connect.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the minimum PC specs?">
    To run Ashburn Creek smoothly, your system should meet at least the following:

    | Component | Minimum |
    |-----------|---------|
    | **CPU** | Intel Core i5 3470 @ 3.2GHz or AMD FX-8350 @ 4GHz |
    | **GPU** | NVIDIA GTX 660 2GB or AMD HD 7870 2GB |
    | **RAM** | 16GB |
    | **Storage** | 72GB free space |

    These are the minimum recommendations. Faster storage (SSD), more RAM, and a stronger GPU all improve loading times, frame rates, and overall stability. If you're on the edge of the minimum specs, expect longer load times and occasional stutters.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a microphone?">
    Yes — a working microphone is **required**. Ashburn Creek uses integrated in-game voice chat, and clear voice communication is a core part of the roleplay experience. You do not need any third-party voice software, but you must be able to speak and be heard clearly by other players. Typed-only roleplay is not supported as a primary communication method.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the age requirement?">
    Ashburn Creek is an **18+ community**. Both you as the player and your character must be 18 years of age or older. Applications from players under 18 will not be accepted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to apply before joining?">
    Yes. You must submit a **whitelist application** through the community Discord before you can connect to the server. Your application confirms that you have read and accepted all server rules. Once approved, you'll receive access to connect and begin character creation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I create a character?">
    Character creation happens during your first-time setup when you connect after being whitelisted. Keep these guidelines in mind:

    - Choose an **original, period-appropriate name** — no references to real people, celebrities, or fictional characters from other media.
    - Write a **simple background** that gives your character a reason to be in the frontier and a motivation to meet people.
    - **Leave room to grow.** Your character's story will develop through actual roleplay, so you don't need an elaborate backstory on day one.
    - Keep everything fitting to the **Wild West setting, circa 1900**. Modern concepts, supernatural elements, and out-of-era references are not allowed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I have multiple characters?">
    Yes, you may create more than one character. However, **items and money cannot be shared between your characters** — each character must earn and acquire their own. Transferring wealth or inventory between characters is a rule violation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What kind of roleplay is expected?">
    Ashburn Creek is a **serious, story-driven roleplay community**. The standard expected of every player includes:

    - Play scenes that are **realistic and lore-friendly**, fitting the Red Dead setting.
    - **Stay in character** during active roleplay. OOC comments should be kept to a minimum and clearly marked.
    - **Create interaction before conflict.** You must have legitimate prior roleplay with another player before attacking or injuring them.
    - Respect **Fear RP** — your character values their life and reacts realistically to danger.
    - Avoid **metagaming** (using out-of-character information to influence in-character decisions).

    Review the full [Roleplay Rules](/rules/roleplay-rules) page for detailed guidance on combat, death, corruption, and more.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I play solo?">
    Absolutely. Many players start as independent, unaffiliated characters and build relationships, find work, or form factions entirely through organic roleplay. You don't need to join a group or faction before joining — just show up, stay in character, and let the story find you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do I do if I have connection or loading issues?">
    Start with these quick checks:

    1. Restart RedM completely.
    2. Make sure both Red Dead Redemption 2 and RedM are fully updated.
    3. Check the community Discord for any server status announcements or scheduled downtime.

    If those steps don't resolve the problem, visit the [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting) page for a full guide. If you're still stuck after working through those steps, open a support ticket in Discord and include the error message, when it occurred, and any screenshots.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I report a rule violation?">
    Open a **support ticket in the community Discord**. Do not DM staff members directly — all reports must go through the ticket system so they can be tracked and reviewed properly. When filing a report, include your character name, the name of the player involved, what occurred, and any supporting screenshots or video clips.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
