The primary way to reach Ashburn Creek staff is through the **ticket system in the community Discord**. This keeps every request logged, visible to the full staff team, and handled in the order it was received. Staff are volunteers who give their time to keep the community running — please be patient and respectful when submitting a request.

<Warning>
  **Do not DM staff members directly.** Unsolicited direct messages to staff will not be answered. All support requests, reports, and applications must go through the Discord ticket system so they can be tracked and resolved properly.
</Warning>

## When to Open a Ticket

Use the ticket system any time you need help that isn't answered by the [FAQ](/support/faq) or [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting) pages. The most common reasons to open a ticket are listed below.

## What to Include in Your Ticket

The more context you provide upfront, the faster your ticket can be resolved. Every ticket should include:

- **Your Discord username** (and tag if applicable)
- **Your character's full name** in Ashburn Creek
- **A clear description of the issue** — what happened, what you expected to happen, and what you tried already
- **Screenshots or video clips** where relevant (especially for rule violations, inventory bugs, or technical errors)
- **The approximate date and time** the issue occurred

<Tip>
  For rule violation reports, video evidence is far more effective than screenshots alone. Even a short clip captured with your system's screen recorder (Windows: `Win + G`; NVIDIA: `Alt + Z`) carries much more weight than a description.
</Tip>

## Whitelist Applications

Before you can connect to Ashburn Creek for the first time, you must submit a **whitelist application through Discord**. Your application confirms that you have read and agreed to all server rules. Look for the application channel in the Discord server and follow the instructions posted there.

<Info>
  Whitelist applications are reviewed by staff volunteers. Response times vary depending on the volume of applications. Submitting multiple applications or messaging staff to ask about the status of your application will not speed up the process.
</Info>

## A Note on Staff Decisions

Staff decisions are **final**. If a staff member rules on a situation — whether it's a ticket outcome, a ban, or an in-game ruling — that decision is not subject to community debate or override by personal interpretations of fairness. If you believe a decision was made in error, you may open a follow-up ticket to appeal calmly and respectfully. Do not raise disputes publicly in community channels.
