Ashburn Creek is a popular server, and when it fills up, new connections are placed in a queue until a slot opens. Queue priority determines where you land in that line — players with higher priority are placed closer to the front and get in faster. If you hold an eligible role or have earned priority through another route, you should not be waiting at the back with everyone else.

## How the Queue Works

When the server reaches capacity, RedM places incoming connections into a queue. Your position in that queue is determined by your total priority points. The more points you hold, the earlier your position, and the shorter your wait.

Priority points from multiple eligible sources **stack** — holding more than one qualifying role or package gives you a combined total that improves your position further.

<Info>
  Staff manages and updates eligible priority through the community Discord. Changes to your roles or packages are reflected in the queue the next time you connect.
</Info>

## Ways to Earn Priority

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Active Whitelist Role">
    Certain whitelisted community roles come with built-in queue priority. If you hold an active role as a **law officer**, **doctor**, or **lawyer**, you receive priority automatically as part of that position.

    Priority tied to a whitelist role is active as long as you hold the role. If you leave or lose the role, the associated priority is removed. Ensure your role is correctly assigned in Discord — that is what the system checks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Priority Package (Store or Donation)">
    You can purchase a priority package directly through the community store or donation page. This grants you a set number of priority points separate from any role you may hold.

    Packages may include their own terms or duration. Review the details at the time of purchase. If you buy a package and do not see your priority reflected after a reasonable period, open a support ticket.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Server Event Reward">
    Staff occasionally awards priority as a reward for participating in or winning community server events. If you earned priority through an event, it is applied by staff after the event concludes.

    Event rewards may include specific terms or a limited duration. Check any announcements related to the event for full details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How Points Stack

Your queue position is calculated from your **combined priority total** across all eligible sources. For example:

- A player holding an active law role **and** a purchased priority package holds points from both sources.
- A player with an event reward **and** a whitelist role stacks those points together.
- A player with no priority sources connects from the standard queue position.

<Tip>
  If you are regularly joining during busy hours, combining an eligible whitelist role with a priority package gives you the strongest queue position available.
</Tip>

## Missing or Incorrect Priority

If your priority does not appear when you connect — or your queue position does not reflect the priority you should hold — open a support ticket in the community Discord.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a Support Ticket">
    Navigate to the ticketing channel in the Ashburn Creek Discord and open a new ticket. Do not DM staff directly — all support requests go through the ticket system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include Your Details">
    In your ticket, provide the following so staff can investigate quickly:

    - Your **Discord name** (including the tag or handle)
    - The **active role or source** you believe qualifies you for priority (e.g. "active doctor role" or "purchased priority package on [date]")
    - **Proof** of your entitlement — a screenshot of your role, a purchase confirmation, or an event reward notification
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Staff to Review">
    Staff will review your ticket and correct your priority if the entitlement is confirmed. Do not open multiple tickets for the same issue — one ticket gives staff everything they need.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Priority is not applied retroactively to time already spent in queue. Once an issue is resolved, your corrected priority takes effect on your next connection attempt.
</Warning>
