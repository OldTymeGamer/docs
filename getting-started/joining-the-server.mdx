Getting into Ashburn Creek takes a few straightforward steps: confirm your PC can run the game, install the right software, submit a whitelist application through Discord, and connect once you are approved. This page walks you through each stage so you arrive in the frontier ready to ride.

## PC Requirements

Before you download anything, make sure your machine meets the minimum specifications for a stable RedM experience.

| Component   | Minimum Requirement                                        |
|-------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| **CPU**     | Intel Core i5 3470 @ 3.2GHz or AMD FX-8350 @ 4GHz         |
| **GPU**     | NVIDIA GTX 660 2GB or AMD HD 7870 2GB                      |
| **RAM**     | 16GB                                                       |
| **Storage** | 72GB free space                                            |

<Tip>
  Faster storage — such as an SSD — and stronger hardware beyond these minimums will noticeably improve loading times and overall stability on the server.
</Tip>

## Software You Need

Ashburn Creek runs on **RedM**, a multiplayer modification framework for Red Dead Redemption 2. You must have both installed before you can connect.

- **Red Dead Redemption 2** — purchased and up to date through your game launcher.
- **RedM** — download the client from [redm.gg](https://redm.gg) and follow the installer instructions. RedM sits alongside your existing RDR2 installation; it does not replace it.

## Microphone Requirement

A working microphone is **required** to play on Ashburn Creek. Voice communication is a core part of frontier roleplay — all in-character interaction happens through live voice, in English. Make sure your microphone is configured and tested in your operating system before you connect for the first time. Once in-game, press **J** to cycle your voice range between Whisper, Normal, and Shout depending on the scene.

## Whitelist Application

Ashburn Creek is a whitelisted community. You must submit an application and receive approval before the server will let you in.

<Note>
  Ashburn Creek is an **18+ community**. Both you and your character must be 18 years of age or older. Applications from players who do not meet this requirement will not be approved.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the Discord">
    Find the official Ashburn Creek Discord invite link from the community's public pages or a trusted community member. Join the server and read through any pinned announcements or welcome channels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the Rules">
    Review the full community rules before you apply. By submitting a whitelist application you are agreeing to follow those rules. Staff will assume you have read them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Your Application">
    Navigate to the whitelist application channel in Discord and complete the application form. Provide accurate information — your application confirms that you accept the server's rules and standards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Approval">
    Staff reviews applications and will update your Discord roles once you are approved. Check your Discord notifications and the relevant channels for a decision. If you have questions, open a support ticket rather than messaging staff directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to the Server">
    Once approved, open RedM and search for **Ashburn Creek** in the server browser, or paste the direct connect address into RedM's connect field. Select the server and join — you are now on your way into the frontier.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your First Connection

When you load in for the first time, you will go through a brief first-time setup to create your character. Keep these points in mind before you begin:

- Choose an **original, period-appropriate name** — no real people, no fictional characters, and nothing that falls outside the Wild West circa 1900 setting.
- Have a simple backstory ready. You do not need every detail figured out; a clear motivation and a reason to meet other people is enough.
- Start slowly once you are in the world. Explore the town, introduce yourself to other players, and let the story build naturally.

<Warning>
  Do not log out during active roleplay or medical scenes. Combat logging is a rule violation and can result in disciplinary action from staff.
</Warning>

Ready to build your character? See [Creating Your Character](/getting-started/creating-your-character) for a full guide on crafting your backstory and arriving prepared for your first day in Ashburn Creek.
