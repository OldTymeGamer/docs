Ashburn Creek is a story-driven RedM community set in the Wild West, circa 1900. Built on Red Dead Redemption 2, it brings together committed players who want more than gunfights — you create grounded, character-led stories shaped by relationships, consequences, and the hard realities of frontier life. Every scene you play, every alliance you forge, and every loss you carry forward becomes part of a living world that only exists because the people in it choose to make it real. If you are ready to commit to a character and let their story breathe, Ashburn Creek is where you belong.

## Start Your Journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the Rules">
    Start with the [Community Standards](/rules/community-standards), then work through the [Roleplay Rules](/rules/roleplay-rules) and [Zero-Tolerance Policies](/rules/zero-tolerance). Knowing the rules before you apply means fewer surprises once you are in the world.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Your Character">
    Choose an original, period-appropriate name — nothing that references real people or fictional characters. Write a short backstory that gives your character a reason to be in the area and room to grow. Review the [Creating Your Character](/getting-started/creating-your-character) guide for detailed guidance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply for Whitelist">
    Submit your whitelist application through the community Discord. Your application confirms that you have read and accepted the rules. Once approved, you will receive access to the server. See [Joining the Server](/getting-started/joining-the-server) for the full process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the World">
    Connect through RedM, which requires an owned copy of Red Dead Redemption 2. Start slowly — meet people in town, learn the lay of the land, and ease into scenes with other players. Your story builds from the first conversation you have.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before You Ride

A few essentials to keep in mind from day one:

- **Microphone required.** Proximity voice chat is the primary means of in-character communication. You cannot participate without one.
- **English only.** All in-character and out-of-character communication must be in English.
- **IC information stays IC.** What your character knows and what you know as a player are two different things. Do not let out-of-character knowledge influence in-character decisions.
- **Use the ticket system.** Do not DM staff directly. Open a support ticket for rule questions, disputes, or anything you are unsure about. Check the [FAQ](/support/faq) first — your question may already be answered.

<Note>
  Ashburn Creek is an **18+ community**. Both you and your character must be 18 or older to participate. A **working microphone** is mandatory — voice communication is central to every roleplay scene and cannot be substituted with text.
</Note>
