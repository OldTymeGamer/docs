Ashburn Creek is a story-driven community. Every rule on this page exists to protect immersion, fairness, and the quality of roleplay for everyone around you. Read each section before you log in, and revisit them whenever you step into an unfamiliar situation. When in doubt, lean toward character-driven interaction over conflict.

## Combat & Conflict

These rules govern how and when violence can occur between players.

- **Roleplay over gunplay:** Build a storyline first. Legitimate, meaningful interaction is required before you attack another player. Do not use violence as a shortcut.
- **RDM (Random Death Match):** You may not kill or injure another player without prior interaction and a valid in-character reason. Killing someone on sight without cause is never acceptable.
- **VDM (Vehicle Death Match):** You may not intentionally run over other players with a cart, horse, or any other vehicle.
- **Baiting:** Do not create fake crimes or behave unrealistically simply to force a law enforcement response or trigger a conflict.
- **Vigilantism:** Do not interfere with active law enforcement scenes or criminal conflicts that you are not already a part of.
- **Hogtie rules:** Do not break free from ropes unless the situation genuinely allows for it. Respect the physical constraints placed on your character.
- **Duels:** Accepting a duel means accepting the consequences that were stated before it began. You cannot dispute the outcome after the fact.

<Warning>
  RDM and VDM are among the most common reasons players are removed from the server. Always establish in-character context before any act of violence.
</Warning>

## Immersion & Fairness

These rules protect the integrity of the roleplay environment and prevent unfair advantages.

- **Powergaming:** Do not exploit bugs, glitches, or unintended game mechanics to force outcomes or gain an advantage over other players.
- **Metagaming:** Do not use information from outside the game — including streams, out-of-character chat, or knowledge your character could not reasonably have — to influence in-character decisions.
- **Fear RP / NVL (No Value of Life):** Value your character's life and act realistically in dangerous situations. A character who ignores a gun pointed at them, or who taunts armed enemies without consequence, is breaking immersion for everyone.
- **Fail RP:** Keep all speech and actions era-appropriate for the Wild West, circa 1900. Modern language, slang, and supernatural roleplay are not allowed under any circumstances.
- **Voice identification:** Respect another player's attempt to mask their identity when they are wearing a mask. Do not use out-of-character knowledge to identify a masked character by their voice.
- **Tonics / revive stims:** Do not use tonics or revive stims during active PvP conflict to re-enter a fight you were losing. These items exist for recovery, not for gaining a mid-fight advantage.
- **Horse theft:** If your horse is stolen in-character, do not use the map or your whistle to magically track or reclaim it. Respond to the theft through roleplay.
- **Body dumping:** If your character disposes of a body, do so in a believable, era-appropriate way. Dumping bodies into water or in obviously unrealistic locations is not permitted.
- **Law and doctor corruption:** Playing a corrupt law officer or corrupt doctor is only permitted after receiving staff approval through a ticket.

<Note>
  Metagaming includes using information you see on streams or in OOC channels. Keep what your character knows and what you know as a player separate at all times.
</Note>

## Death & Consequences

These rules define how your character experiences death, injury, and the aftermath of conflict.

- **Revenge killing:** If your character is killed, do not return to the same scene seeking revenge. Stay away from that area for at least one hour after the incident.
- **New Life Rule:** If you respawn after dying, your character forgets the specific scenario that led to their death. You may not use knowledge of that event to inform your next actions.
- **Medical roleplay:** Stay honest and realistic during all medical scenes. Do not rush through them, exaggerate injuries, or falsely claim recovery to re-enter a conflict.
- **Perma death:** Permanent character death is your own decision unless a specific server rule states otherwise. No other player can force a perma on your character without your consent.
- **Combat logging:** Do not log out of the server during an active roleplay scene, a conflict, or a medical situation. Leaving to avoid consequences is a serious breach of trust.

<Tip>
  If you need to log off urgently during an active scene, let the other players know in OOC so the situation can be resolved fairly. Disappearing without a word is still considered combat logging.
</Tip>

## Items & Economy

- **Items and money:** Do not transfer items or money between your own characters. Each character exists independently, and cross-character trading undermines the economy and the integrity of your stories.
