Ashburn Creek holds a small number of rules to a higher standard than the rest. The behaviours listed on this page cause serious harm — to individual players, to the community's safety, or to the integrity of the roleplay environment itself. They are not matters of interpretation, and they do not receive the same case-by-case leniency as other rule violations.

<Warning>
  **Violations of any zero-tolerance policy may result in an automatic permanent ban.** Staff retains the discretion to determine the outcome in any specific case, but you should treat every item on this page as a potential end to your time on Ashburn Creek.
</Warning>

## Zero-Tolerance Policies

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Exploiting">
    Do not use game exploits, glitches, or any unintended mechanics to gain an advantage, grief other players, or disrupt the server. This applies whether the exploit is well-known or newly discovered. If you find a bug, report it through a ticket — do not use it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Discriminatory Language or Actions">
    Do not use slurs, derogatory remarks, or discriminatory language of any kind. This includes language targeting a person's race, ethnicity, national origin, sexual orientation, gender identity, religion, or disability. There is no in-character justification that makes this acceptable.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Sexual Harassment">
    Do not direct unwanted or inappropriate comments, actions, or advances of a sexual nature toward any member of the community — in-game, on Discord, or anywhere else. Consent does not make this permissible in public community spaces.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Stalking or Predatory Behaviour">
    Do not engage in creepy, threatening, or unwarranted attention toward any member. This includes repeated unwanted contact, following someone in-game with the intent to intimidate, or any behaviour that makes another person feel unsafe.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Suicide Roleplay">
    Any depiction, discussion, or roleplay involving suicide is strictly prohibited. This rule exists to protect every member of the community. There are no exceptions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Third-Party Communication">
    Keep all in-character communication inside the game. Do not use Discord calls, external voice software, or any third-party platform to pass in-character information during active gameplay. This rule overlaps with metagaming — both apply.

    <Note>
      Out-of-character coordination with your group is different from passing IC advantage through third-party tools. If you are unsure where the line is, submit a ticket before your session.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Roleplay Bleed">
    Do not allow out-of-character emotions, frustrations, or personal conflicts to negatively affect your in-character roleplay or your treatment of other players. What happens between characters stays between characters. If you are struggling to separate yourself from a scene, step away and take a break.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How to Report a Zero-Tolerance Violation

If you witness or experience any of the behaviours described on this page, submit a ticket immediately. Do not confront the person yourself, do not post about it in public channels, and do not attempt to gather your own evidence through rule-breaking means. Let staff handle it.

<Tip>
  Save screenshots or recordings before submitting your ticket. Clear evidence makes it significantly easier for staff to act quickly and fairly.
</Tip>
