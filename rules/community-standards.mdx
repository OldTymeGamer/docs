Ashburn Creek is a community built on mutual respect, good-faith roleplay, and a shared commitment to the frontier experience. These standards apply to every member — in-game, on Discord, and anywhere else the community gathers. Read them carefully before you apply, and return to them whenever you are unsure how to act.

## Agreement and Enforcement

By submitting a whitelist application, you agree to follow these rules in full. Rules may be updated at any time, so it is your responsibility to stay informed of any changes. When a situation arises that the rules do not explicitly cover, staff will assess it on a case-by-case basis. Staff decisions are final.

<Note>
  If you are ever unsure whether an action is allowed, create a ticket and ask before you act. Getting clarity first protects you and the community.
</Note>

## Age Requirement

<Warning>
  Ashburn Creek is strictly an **18+ community**. Both you and your character must be 18 years of age or older. Anyone found to be under 18 will be removed immediately.
</Warning>

## Character Names

Use an original character name that fits the Wild West setting of circa 1900. Do not reference real public figures, celebrities, or recognizable fictional characters. Keep your character name — and all roleplay — era-appropriate.

## General Conduct

These rules govern how you interact with every other member of the community.

- **Respect and conduct:** Treat all members with respect at all times. Harassment, trolling, sexism, racism, and hate speech are not tolerated.
- **Political topics:** Do not discuss politically charged or inflammatory topics in any community space.
- **Spam and self-promotion:** Do not spam channels or promote outside content without explicit approval from staff.
- **NSFW content:** NSFW or obscene content is strictly prohibited in all community spaces.
- **Account security:** Do not share your Steam account with anyone else.
- **Rule acknowledgment:** You must read and understand all rules before entering the server.
- **Toxic attitude:** Do not bring out-of-character toxicity, frustration, or personal conflicts into in-character spaces or community channels. Keep the community environment positive for everyone.

## Reporting Issues

If you encounter rule-breaking behaviour or feel unsafe at any time, submit a ticket through the proper channel. Do not attempt to handle rule violations yourself.

<Tip>
  The ticket system is the right tool for every support request — from rule questions to reporting a player. Using it keeps a clear record and gets you help faster.
</Tip>

## Discord and Staff Communication

- **Discord nickname:** Your Discord nickname must reflect your primary character name at all times.
- **Contacting staff:** Do not DM staff members directly unless they specifically request it. Use the ticket system for all support and enquiries.

## Gameplay Restrictions

- **Macros:** Do not use macros of any kind during gameplay.
- **Third-party crosshairs:** Do not use third-party crosshair overlays or aim-assistance tools.
- **AFK behaviour:** Log out of the server if you need to step away. Being AFK does not exempt you from ongoing interactions or consequences.

## Communication Standards

- **Language:** All in-game and community communication must be in English.
- **Microphone:** A working microphone is required to play on Ashburn Creek. You cannot participate meaningfully in voice-driven frontier roleplay without one.
