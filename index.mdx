Ashburn Creek is a whitelist RedM roleplay community where every player builds a grounded frontier story through character-led scenes. Whether you ride in as a rancher, a lawman, a doctor, or a wanderer, your choices shape the world around you. If you are ready to leave modern life behind and live out a life on the frontier, this is where your story begins.

## Your First Steps in Ashburn Creek

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the Rules">
    Review [Community Standards](/rules/community-standards) and [Roleplay Rules](/rules/roleplay-rules) before submitting your application. Knowing the rules is a condition of membership.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply for Whitelist">
    Submit a whitelist application through the community Discord. Your application confirms that you accept the server rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Your Character">
    Choose an original, period-appropriate name and write a simple backstory. Leave room for your character to grow through play.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ride In">
    Connect, start slowly, and meet the people of Ashburn Creek. Build relationships, find work, and let your story unfold.
  </Step>
</Steps>
